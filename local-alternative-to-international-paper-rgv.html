What Continuity-of-Supply Onboarding Looks Like at ABBA

1. Spec Review

Bring your existing print files, structural drawings, and a sample of the box you currently buy. ABBA’s production engineer reviews the specs and confirms what can be replicated as-is and what needs adjustment for our presses and die-cutting capacity.

2. Validation Run

A small validation batch is produced for your QC team to sign off. Print color, board grade, structural performance, and gluing integrity are all verified against your acceptance criteria before full production begins.

3. Full Production Transition

Once validation is signed off, ABBA transitions to your normal volume cadence. ISO 9001:2015 documentation accompanies each shipment to support procurement and quality audits.

4. Local Lead Times

Most RGV deliveries are 1 to 3 business days from the Pharr facility. Same-day delivery is available within a 25-mile radius for established accounts. Freight cost drops substantially compared to out-of-region suppliers.

Why a Local Pharr-Based Manufacturer Beats a National Supplier on Total Landed Cost
