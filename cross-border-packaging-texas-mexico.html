Cross-Border Packaging Solutions

Whether your operation assembles components in Reynosa, ships finished goods northbound into the United States, or manages inventory on both sides of the border, ABBA builds the packaging that keeps your supply chain moving.

Location is not a convenience in cross-border manufacturing -- it is a strategic asset. That proximity translates into tangible advantages for every client operating in the binational trade zone.

When a shipment needs to clear the bridge on schedule, the packaging cannot be the variable that causes a delay.

ABBA's bilingual workforce is fluent in English and Spanish, which eliminates the communication gaps that slow down cross-border projects. From initial design consultations to production updates and delivery coordination, your team can work with ours in whichever language is most efficient. That capability extends to the packaging itself -- bilingual print layouts are reviewed internally before going to press, not outsourced to a translation service that may not understand packaging-specific terminology.

Industries Along the Border We Serve

The Texas-Mexico border region supports a concentrated ecosystem of manufacturing, assembly, and distribution operations. ABBA Manufacturing provides corrugated packaging to businesses across this industrial corridor, including facilities on both sides of the Rio Grande.

  • Maquiladora and assembly plants -- corrugated trays, dividers, and shipping boxes for parts staging, work-in-process storage, and finished goods export
  • Automotive parts manufacturers -- heavy-duty containers and protective inserts for engine components, wiring harnesses, and precision-machined parts moving between U.S. and Mexican facilities
  • Electronics manufacturing -- anti-static and cushioned packaging solutions for circuit boards, display assemblies, and consumer electronics produced in border-region plants
  • Food processing and agriculture -- food-safe corrugated packaging for produce, frozen goods, and processed foods crossing the border under USDA and FDA regulations
  • Medical device packaging -- clean, consistent corrugated solutions for medical components and devices manufactured in maquiladora facilities and distributed into U.S. healthcare supply chains

Your Nearshoring Packaging Partner on the U.S. Side
