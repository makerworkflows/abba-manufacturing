Why Brands Choose ABBA for Retail Packaging

The difference between packaging that sells and packaging that sits on a shelf comes down to execution. ABBA Manufacturing delivers on four fronts that matter most to retail and luxury brands.

  • High-quality print and finishing -- Our lithographic and digital printing capabilities produce sharp, color-accurate graphics across every substrate we run. Combined with our in-house finishing department, we deliver packaging that matches brand guidelines down to the Pantone spec.
  • Rigid and folding carton options -- Not every luxury product needs a rigid box, and not every retail product needs a folding carton. We manufacture both formats under one roof, giving brands the flexibility to match packaging structure to product positioning and price point.
  • Short-run capability for launches -- Brands launching new products, testing seasonal lines, or entering new markets need packaging in quantities of hundreds, not hundreds of thousands. ABBA supports short-run production without compromising on print quality or finishing standards.
  • ISO quality consistency -- Our ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system ensures that the packaging you approve during proofing is the packaging you receive at delivery. Color, die-cut accuracy, adhesive strength, and finishing quality are verified at every stage of production.

Finishing Capabilities

The finishing stage is where packaging transforms from printed board into a tactile brand asset. ABBA Manufacturing operates a full finishing department in-house, eliminating the delays and quality risks of outsourcing specialty processes to third-party vendors.

  • Embossing and debossing -- Raised or recessed patterns that add dimension and a premium feel to logos, typography, and decorative elements
  • Hot foil stamping -- Gold, silver, copper, holographic, and custom-color metallic foils applied with precision dies for high-visibility brand marks
  • Spot UV coating -- Selective gloss coating applied to specific areas of the packaging to create contrast against matte backgrounds, drawing the eye to logos or product names
  • Matte and gloss lamination -- Full-surface lamination that protects printed graphics and adds a consistent tactile finish across the entire package
  • Custom inserts -- Die-cut paperboard, molded pulp, EVA foam, and flocked inserts designed to cradle products securely and present them cleanly when the box is opened
