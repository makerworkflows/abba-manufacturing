ABBA Manufacturing is that manufacturer. Based in Pharr, TX, we produce corrugated agricultural packaging built specifically for the demands of fresh produce handling. Food-Contact Packaging and ISO 9001:2015 Quality

Agricultural packaging is food-contact packaging. The corrugated box that holds your citrus or wraps your peppers is the last manufactured product that touches your crop before it reaches the consumer. That is why quality control in agricultural packaging is not optional -- it is a food safety requirement.

ABBA Manufacturing holds ISO 9001:2015 certification, which governs every step of our production process. Raw materials are inspected and verified for food-contact compliance before entering the production line. Board caliper, burst strength, and moisture resistance are tested at defined intervals throughout each run. Wax coating weight and distribution are measured to ensure consistent moisture protection across every panel. Finished packaging undergoes dimensional verification and stacking strength testing before release. Every lot is traceable from raw material receipt through final shipment, giving our customers the documentation they need to satisfy buyer audits and food safety programs.

For growers and packers who sell into retail chains, foodservice distributors, or export markets, having an ISO-certified packaging supplier is increasingly a requirement -- not a differentiator. ABBA Manufacturing gives you that certification backed by real, auditable quality systems running on the production floor every day.
