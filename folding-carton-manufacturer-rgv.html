Folding Carton Capabilities

Straight Tuck End Cartons

The most common folding carton style, ideal for pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, food products, and general retail packaging.

Reverse Tuck End Cartons

Tuck flaps open from opposite ends for easier filling on automated packaging lines. Common in food and hardware applications.

Auto-Bottom Cartons

Pre-glued bottom panels snap into place during assembly, delivering faster packing speeds for high-volume operations.

Custom Die-Cut Cartons

Unique shapes and window cutouts designed to your product dimensions. Includes inserts, partitions, and specialty closures.
