Ordering custom shipping boxes from an East Coast or overseas manufacturer means longer lead times, higher freight costs, and zero ability to walk the production floor when something needs adjusting. ABBA Manufacturing is located in Pharr, TX, which puts us within a one- to two-day ground shipping window for most of the state. The cost of wrong-sized boxes: Shipping a product in a box that is two inches too wide in every direction means paying for void fill material, absorbing dimensional weight surcharges from carriers, and increasing the risk of product damage from shifting during transit. Custom sizing from ABBA eliminates all three problems.

Beyond sizing, ABBA Manufacturing offers full print capability on shipping boxes. Branded packaging is no longer a luxury reserved for Fortune 500 companies. Every shipping box produced at ABBA Manufacturing passes through our ISO 9001:2015 quality management system. Quality and Customization at Every Level

ABBA Manufacturing does not offer a one-size-fits-all catalog. Every shipping box project starts with your product dimensions, weight, fragility, and shipping method. From there, we recommend the appropriate flute type, board grade, closure style, and print approach. Single-wall corrugated handles most e-commerce and light industrial applications. Double-wall and triple-wall constructions are available for heavy parts, machinery, and products that require stacking strength during warehousing or palletized freight.

Customization extends beyond the box itself. We produce die-cut inserts, corrugated partitions, and foam-lined interiors that cradle your product during transit. For businesses shipping fragile or high-value goods, fitted interior packaging dramatically reduces damage rates and return costs. Combined with a properly sized outer shipper, the result is a packaging system that protects your product and your margins.
