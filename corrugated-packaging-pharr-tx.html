What We Manufacture

RSC (Regular Slotted Container) Boxes

The industry standard for shipping and storage. All four flaps meet at the center when closed, providing uniform protection. Available in single-wall, double-wall, and triple-wall configurations for any weight class.

Die-Cut Corrugated Containers

Precision-cut corrugated containers designed to fit exact product dimensions. Includes self-locking tabs, interlocking partitions, and custom cutouts for retail display, product protection, or automated packing lines.

Heavy-Duty Industrial Packaging

Double-wall and triple-wall corrugated solutions for oversized, high-weight, or fragile industrial goods. Engineered for maximum edge crush resistance and stacking strength during long-haul transport and warehouse storage.

Custom Print Corrugated Boxes

Branded corrugated packaging with flexographic printing for logos, handling instructions, barcodes, and compliance labeling. Full-color and spot-color options available for retail-facing and wholesale applications.

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Controls for Corrugated Packaging

ABBA Manufacturing holds ISO 9001:2015 certification, and our corrugated packaging production follows controlled quality procedures at every stage. Incoming corrugated board is inspected for caliper thickness, flute integrity, and moisture content before entering production. Die-cut accuracy is verified against approved CAD drawings. Edge crush test (ECT) and burst strength values are validated against the load specifications defined for each order. Print registration, adhesive bond strength, and final box dimensions are checked before any shipment is released.

This level of process control means that a corrugated box ordered today will perform identically to the same box ordered six months from now. For businesses in the Rio Grande Valley that depend on consistent packaging performance -- whether shipping produce from Weslaco, distributing manufactured goods from McAllen, or supplying retail operations in Brownsville -- ABBA's quality systems remove the uncertainty that comes with working with uncertified suppliers.
